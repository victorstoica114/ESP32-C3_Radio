# E79 TX recapture bench notes

## Lot B detector qualification and preserved attempts

- `lot270_other_profiles_consensus` stopped after 10 preserved RAW captures at `run_00007` (`GFSK4K8`, -20 dBm, 512 B, repetition 2). The 1 ms detector found 8 frames at 3 MAD but only 7 at the mandatory 4 MAD threshold. No rows were reused in the final lot.
- Long modeled frames now use adaptive 1-2 ms averaging while retaining at least 64 bins per shortest frame. GFSK200 remains on 1 ms bins. Offline reanalysis made the original failing GFSK4K8 trace valid at 4/3/5 MAD and left all 45 final GFSK200 traces valid with 1 ms bins.
- `lot270_other_profiles_adaptive_bins` stopped automatically after 10 preserved RAW captures. Three rows were rejected because the configured 5 ms merge gap had accidentally become only one inactive 2 ms bin after adaptive binning. `_groups` compares active-index distance, so the implementation now includes the endpoint and preserves the requested duration in milliseconds. A focused regression test covers a 4 ms internal dip with 2 ms bins.
- `lot270_other_profiles_strict_multiscale` stopped automatically after 12 preserved RAW captures at a weak 16-frame GFSK4K8 trace. Its exact 16 windows and energy were stable from 2 through 2.75 MAD, with zero pre-trigger frame-like groups, while higher thresholds fragmented the low-current plateaus.
- The long-frame low-signal fallback is restricted to adaptive (>1 ms) bins and requires all four 2/2.25/2.5/2.75-MAD detectors to independently pass count and QC with energy agreement within 1%. It uses the 2.5-MAD windows. It is never available to short GFSK200/GFSK50/IEEE frames. The previously ambiguous 1 ms GFSK200 capture remains rejected.
- `lot270_other_profiles_long_frame_consensus` stopped automatically after 85 preserved RAW captures at `GFSK50`, 13 dBm, 512 B. The peer received the transfer. A low-current setup shoulder made the first 4-MAD group overlong; every threshold from 4.5 through 8 MAD found the same 8 physical plateaus and identical energy.
- The high-signal fallback requires the complete 4.5/5/5.5-MAD bracket to independently pass exact count, detector QC and the existing 1% energy-consensus limit. It uses the 5-MAD windows. It cannot recover a weak frame that disappears at higher thresholds.
- `lot270_other_profiles_dual_fallback_consensus` stopped after 60 valid RAW captures when a single unmeasured warm-up packet was not received during a parameter transition. This was an initialization false stop rather than a measured radio result.
- Warm-up now retries only the unmeasured verification transfer up to three times. Every measured capture remains single-shot: it is never retried or removed, and `rx_missing` remains a valid preserved result. Three consecutive warm-up misses still raise a terminal exception. Two focused tests cover recovery and terminal exhaustion.
- `lot270_other_profiles_final_candidate` stopped before its first capture on a transient COM16 write timeout during initial configuration. The empty attempt and console log are preserved. COM16 then returned `E79_AT_MODEM,0.3.0,SimpleLink-LPF2-SDK 8.33.00.16` and `OK` at 1 Mbaud. The final handoff used 6 s both for PPK2 re-enumeration and DUT boot stabilization.
